# ⚔️ Brutal Ages

🇨🇿 [Česky](README.md) · 🇬🇧 **English**

**Brutal ages demand brutal wars.**

A brutal war through the ages, from caves to space mechs. Build an army, defend your base, evolve and crush your opponent – alone against the computer, in the campaign, or online with friends.

### ▶ [Play in the browser](https://aeloth666.github.io/brutal-ages/) · 📲 [Download for Android (APK)](https://aeloth666.github.io/brutal-ages/BrutalAges.apk)

Once installed, the app updates itself – new game versions are downloaded at startup.

---

## What's in the game

- **5 ages** – stone age, middle ages, renaissance, modern age and the future, each with its own units, towers, special attack and hero.
- **20 nations** – 4 in every age (e.g. Wolf Tribe, Vikings, Samurai, Pirates, Aliens, Robots…), each with its own bonus, look, base, war cry and **special unit** (ninja, berserker, priest, saboteur, parasite…); in the stone age every tribe rides its own animal (mammoth, wolf, bear, aurochs).
- **Campaign** – 15 territories across history, **bosses** at the end of every age (sabre-toothed tiger, dragon, Leonardo's armoured car, giant tank, titan) and the Red Horde that sometimes attacks territories you have conquered.
- **🎖 Headquarters** – profile, general, quests, achievements, nations, statistics and **👥 friends** (player ID, search by name, friend requests, messages and stats comparison) in one place; a badge shows what is new.
- **General** – a skill tree with three branches (Army, Defence, Economy), bonuses from conquered territories and hero gear.
- **✅ Quests** – daily and weekly quests that give general points and **army skins**.
- **Battlefields with features** – meadow, desert with sandstorms, winter, volcano with lava, swamp and forest; long battles turn into night.
- **Multiplayer** – 1 vs 1 duel, co-op against the computer, 2 vs 2 (also with three players and the computer as a teammate, everyone picks their team; in a team everyone has their own gold and nation), tournaments, invites for recent opponents, **spectator mode** and emotes.
- **🌍 Online leaderboard** – rating (Elo) from online duels of all players, clans.
- **After-battle statistics** – army strength graph and who killed how many and with what.
- Difficulties from easy to **🔥 Insane** (unlocked after finishing the campaign), daily challenge, achievements, profile with ranks.
- **🩸 Brutal mode** for the hardened (and a few secrets you have to find yourself 🥚).
- The game is in **Czech and English** (Settings → Game).

## Controls

On a phone everything is a tap. On a computer you can also use the keyboard:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `1`–`9` | buttons in the bottom bar from the left |
| `H` | hero / crushing blow |
| `Space` | special attack |
| `V` | evolve |
| `T` / `U` | towers / upgrades |
| `Q` `W` `E` | order: attack / hold / retreat |
| `Tab` | speed up the game |
| `Esc` | pause |

## Installing on Android

1. Download [BrutalAges.apk](https://aeloth666.github.io/brutal-ages/BrutalAges.apk).
2. Open it and allow installing from this source (Android asks once).
3. Done – further game updates arrive on their own.

You can also add the game to your home screen straight from the browser (browser menu → *Add to Home screen*) and play offline.
